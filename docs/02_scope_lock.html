2

Locked scope

In scope

  • Patient chat — questions answered from that patient's own record only
  • Clinician dashboard — ranked alerts with drill-down
  • Alert → summary bridge — the contribution with no analogue in prior work
  • Federated detector — sequence models trained across sites
  • Differential privacy — DP-SGD with a privacy budget sweep
  • Lab report ingestion — PDF into the retrieval store
  • Voice input and output — local ASR and TTS, demo scope only

Out of scope

  • Voice as a research contribution — see the note below
  • Meal, exercise and calorie logging
  • Medication and appointment reminders
  • Federated fine-tuning of the language model — not feasible at this scale
  • Interface polish — functional demo only; rigour lives in the experiments
  • EHR / FHIR integration — future work
  • Live clinical deployment — future work
  • PTB-XL validation — demoted to optional; see below
PTB-XL — its justification no longer exists PTB-XL was added for exactly one reason: the primary dataset was synthetic, and a reviewer would object that the finding might be an artefact of simulated data. Validating the trend on real physiological signals answered that objection.

The primary dataset is now real. PhysioNet/CinC 2019 is genuine ICU data from two hospital systems. There is no synthetic-data objection left to defend against, so the original rationale is void.

What remains is a weaker, different claim — that the trend generalises across data modality, from tabular vitals to ECG-derived features. That is a legitimate result, but it costs ~30 training runs (roughly 5.5 days of compute) on a grid already over budget.

Recommended: drop it, and spend the compute on additional seeds instead. That directly fixes the blocking statistical problem — the comparison cannot reach significance at 5 seeds, and can at 6+. One change resolves an open item and reclaims 5.5 days. The P0 mapping work is not wasted: it is recorded, and the experiment can be run later if a reviewer asks for it.
Voice — the boundary that keeps this safe Voice is a product and demo feature only. It does not appear among the contributions, it is not evaluated in the paper, and no result depends on it. Build it after the pipeline works, not before.

A research angle does exist — does speech-to-text error propagate into retrieval failures or hallucinated summaries? — and no prior work in b1–b5 touches it. It is deliberately not being taken: this project already carries four contributions, a two-person team, a paper at zero percent, and an experiment grid over its compute budget. Record it as follow-up work.

Must appear in the paper Forward-fill imputation is now a declared method, not an implementation detail. Report the per-vital imputation rate: temperature is carried forward roughly two hours in three, glucose four in five. Silent imputation at these rates would be a legitimate reviewer objection.
Prediction horizon — the setting that decides whether the paper has a finding Labels are derived from vitals, and vitals barely change hour to hour — worse, forward-fill means a carried value can appear both as model input and as the basis of the label. Predicting the step immediately after the window is therefore closer to reproducing a formula than to forecasting.

MEASURED AUPRC falls from 0.68 at horizon 0 to 0.43 at 6 hours ahead; AUROC from 0.90 to 0.78. The easy version is not measuring clinical prediction.

Two reasons this matters beyond honesty. First, early warning is the entire clinical premise — a monitor that announces deterioration already visible in the current vitals warns nobody. Second, and critical for the research: an easy task leaves no room for the ε-sweep to show anything. If accuracy is high and flat, DP noise degrades it slowly and uniformly, the architectures do not separate, and C1 and C2 both come back null — not because the hypothesis is wrong, but because the task could not discriminate.

Numbers above are single runs on 600 patients with a small model; the horizon-0 gap is large and mechanistically explained, but the difference between 6 and 12 hours is within noise. Confirm the horizon with seeds before locking it.
Splitting — binding Split by patient, everywhere: train/test, any cross-validation (grouped), and the held-out set. Stride-1 windows overlap by 11 of 12 timesteps, so a random window split puts near-duplicates on both sides — 95% of patients appeared in both halves when measured.

Honest note: the measured penalty was negligible (−0.7% AUPRC), because NEWS2 labels are a smooth function of vitals rather than something patient-specific to memorise. Adopt patient-level splitting anyway — the deployed system predicts for patients it has never seen, and this is the first thing a reviewer checks.
Labelling — the strongest version of this argument NEWS2 is a rule-based label, which the Research Guide correctly identified as a weakness. The original mitigation was to cross-check against MEWS — but MEWS is another rule-based scheme, so agreement between them proves only that two rules agree.

This dataset allows something better: cross-checking against clinically-adjudicated sepsis onset in the same records. MEASURED NEWS2 flagged 75% of septic patients in site A and 60% in site B at some point during their stay. Hour-level precision is low by design — NEWS2 is an escalation trigger that fires more broadly than any single condition — but patient-level recall is the meaningful figure, and it shows the derived labels track genuine deterioration.

That converts "rule-based labels" from a limitation into a validated method. Note also that two NEWS2 components — consciousness level and supplemental oxygen — are not numerically recorded here, so the attainable maximum is 14 rather than 20. State this; the standard threshold of 5 still sits well inside the attainable range.

Locked experimental protocol

Privacy accounting — binding protocol

  • Size the noise multiplier once for rounds × local_epochs × steps, then hold it fixed
  • Persist each client's accountant across rounds via state_dict() / load_state_dict()
  • Report the worst-case site's ε, never the mean
  • State the privacy unit explicitly: this is window-level, not patient-level

Metrics

  • AUPRC is the headline metric, with AUC-ROC reported alongside
  • At 12.6% prevalence the imbalance is moderate, so AUC-ROC is informative here — unlike at the 1.5% of the sepsis label, where it would have been actively misleading
  • False negative rate reported separately as the clinical measure
  • F1, precision, recall retained as secondary
  • Every run records achieved ε alongside target ε
  • All results as mean ± std across seeds; never single runs

Compute discipline

  • Full grid measures ~22 days on available hardware — must be trimmed
  • Cut rounds and dataset size before scope: each saves 50%, versus 17–24% for dropping model types
  • Verify convergence by round 50 before paying for 100
  • Dropping PTB-XL reclaims ~5.5 days; redirect it to seeds, which the statistics require
  • Never trade away the seed count — it is a validity constraint, not a scope choice

Summary evaluation

  • Claim-level fact verification is mandatory — CHANGED
  • Keyword matching is proven inadequate: it scored two fabricating models as clean
  • Human rubric with two raters and inter-rater agreement
  • LLM-as-judge as the third layer
The correction that matters most The natural way to write the federated privacy code restarts the accountant every round, reporting a flat ε that under-states privacy loss by 2.43× after only six rounds — and it fails in the flattering direction, so it looks correct. The protocol above is not a preference; without it the paper states a false number.

Open items — decisions still required

Everything above is settled. These are not, and each needs a person rather than a measurement.

1 · Statistical plan for the architecture comparison

  • As specified — pairwise tests at 5 seeds with correction for 12 comparisons — no experimental outcome can reach significance, because the smallest attainable p sits above the corrected threshold
  • Recommended: one omnibus model over architecture × ε, with per-ε effect sizes reported descriptively
  • Blocking, because it determines the seed count and therefore the compute budget

2 · Base paper citations b2 and b3

  • The PRD and Research Guide give different titles for the same tags, and different years for b3 (Dec 2024 vs 2025)
  • Either two different papers are cited under one label, or one document has the reference wrong
  • Resolve by pulling up the actual papers before the related-work section is written

3 · Number of federated clients

  • Two real hospital systems is the honest minimum
  • They differ in measurement practice rather than patient population — glucose recorded 11.8% vs 21.2% of hours
  • Subdividing by ICU unit or age band gives more clients and stronger heterogeneity, at the cost of the "real institutions" claim

4 · Mentor sign-off on the dataset switch

  • This reverses what the Prototype Guide decided, so it should be agreed rather than presented as done
  • The evidence is strong: Synthea's 154-day gaps and absent SpO2 versus PhysioNet's 86% coverage and provided labels
